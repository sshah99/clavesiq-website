Most AI projects fail between the demo and production. We don't build demos. We architect systems where the model is one component in a larger, observable, testable pipeline — with retrieval that stays current, tools that don't drift, evals that catch regressions before users do, and guardrails that hold in edge cases. Whether you need a copilot inside your product, an autonomous agent processing your operations queue, or a decision-support layer on top of clinical or financial data — we've shipped it, and we know what breaks after launch.

Decisions move at the speed of your data infrastructure. We design and build modern data stacks — lakehouses, warehouses, ELT pipelines, semantic layers, and analytics products — that teams actually use. Our work spans Snowflake, Databricks, dbt, Airflow, and Dagster. We embed data quality checks and observability from day one, not as an afterthought.

Cloud infrastructure done wrong becomes the ceiling on everything else — your AI can't scale, your data can't move fast enough, and your compliance posture becomes a liability. We build AWS-first foundations that are secure by design, not by retrofit: HIPAA-eligible landing zones, network isolation, encryption at the tenant level, and infrastructure-as-code from day one. A practical path from idea to production.

We do not start with a giant discovery deck. We start with the decision, workflow, data, risk, and success metric — then build the smallest production-shaped system that can prove value with real users. For AI and regulated data, disciplined matters more than rapid.

SEC. 04 — HOW WE WORK
STEP
01

Frame the Decision

We identify the workflow, user, data sources, risk level, and business metric. We also map where AI or automation should not be used — that boundary matters as much as the solution.

STEP
02

Prove the System

We prototype against real data, define evaluation criteria, test edge cases, and decide what must be automated, reviewed by a human, or rejected outright before anything gets built for production.

STEP
03

Ship the Workflow

We build the product surface, APIs, data pipelines, cloud infrastructure, observability, security controls, and release path. Real users early — not after months of isolated build time.

STEP
04

Operate and Improve

We monitor quality, cost, drift, adoption, exceptions, and business outcomes — then tune the system with the teams who own it. We stay around long enough to defend the metrics we promised.
