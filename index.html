SEC. 03 · HEALTH & LIFE SCIENCES
H.01
Claims, Payer & Provider Data Systems

We engineer cloud pipelines and governed data models for medical and pharmacy claims, eligibility, remittance, payer, plan, NPI, provider, and facility data. Entity resolution, validation, anomaly detection, and machine learning reconcile fragmented sources, identify exceptions, and support reliable claims analytics and workflow automation.

H.02
Clinical AI & EHR Engineering

We build interoperability and AI pipelines that ingest HL7 v2, FHIR, API, and document-based data from clinical systems. Clinical NLP and document intelligence can structure notes, extract relevant information, normalize terminology, and support downstream workflows. Every output includes provenance, evaluation, security controls, and appropriate human review.

H.03
Market Access Data & Intelligence

We create governed data layers across claims, formulary, coverage, transaction, hub, specialty-pharmacy, and contract data. Identity resolution, taxonomy alignment, lineage, feature engineering, and AI models turn these sources into dependable intelligence for access barriers, formulary changes, launch planning, field workflows, and patient services.
